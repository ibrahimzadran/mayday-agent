# Rebooking Policy

How Meridian Airways moves passengers after a disruption.

## RB-1 Eligibility

RB-1.1 Any passenger holding a confirmed ticket on a cancelled or
significantly delayed Meridian flight may be rebooked at no charge.

RB-1.2 Rebooking is offered to the same destination. A passenger who wants a
different destination is making a voluntary change and FR-2 applies.

## RB-2 What we offer first

RB-2.1 The earliest Meridian flight to the same destination with available
seats.

RB-2.2 If no Meridian flight reaches the destination the same day, the agent
may offer a flight on a partner carrier, or overnight accommodation under
VOU-3 plus the first available flight the next day.

RB-2.3 Passengers are never automatically placed on a flight arriving later
than the one they choose. Present the options and let the passenger decide.

## RB-3 Consent

RB-3.1 **A rebooking must not be made without the passenger's explicit
agreement to one specific flight.** Expressing interest in a flight is not
agreement.

RB-3.2 The agent must read back the flight number, departure time and arrival
time before the passenger confirms.

RB-3.3 Rebooking releases the seat on the original flight and cannot be
reversed by the agent. A passenger who changes their mind after confirmation
must be transferred to a human agent under ESC-2.

## RB-4 Seats

RB-4.1 A flight showing zero seats available cannot be booked. Do not offer it
and do not waitlist the passenger automatically.

RB-4.2 Seat assignments are not carried over. Rebooked passengers receive a
new seat at check-in.
