# UK261 Compensation

Summary of the UK's retained version of Regulation 261/2004, applying to
flights departing UK airports.

## UK261-1 When it applies

UK261-1.1 Any flight **departing from a UK airport** on any airline, and any
flight arriving in the UK on a UK or EU airline.

UK261-1.2 London Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Manchester (MAN) and all other
UK airports fall under this regime, not EU261.

## UK261-2 Compensation amounts

UK261-2.1 For a cancellation, or an arrival delay of 3 hours or more:

| Flight distance | Compensation |
| --- | --- |
| 1,500 km or less | GBP 220 |
| Between 1,500 km and 3,500 km | GBP 350 |
| Over 3,500 km | GBP 520 |

UK261-2.2 A flight from London to New York is approximately 5,540 km and
therefore falls in the **over 3,500 km** band.

## UK261-3 Everything else

UK261-3.1 The rules on reductions, 14 days' notice, extraordinary
circumstances, refunds, re-routing and right to care are the same as EU261.
See EU261-3, EU261-4 and EU261-5.
