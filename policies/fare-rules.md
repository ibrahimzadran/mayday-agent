# Fare Rules

Meridian Airways fare families and what each one permits.

## FR-1 Fare families

| Fare class | Changes | Refundable | Seat selection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BASIC_ECONOMY | Not permitted | No | At check-in only |
| ECONOMY_BASIC | Fee applies | No | Included |
| MAIN_CABIN | Fee applies | No | Included |
| ECONOMY_FLEX | Free | Yes, to original payment | Included |
| PREMIUM_ECONOMY | Free | Yes, to original payment | Included |
| BUSINESS | Free | Yes, to original payment | Included |

## FR-2 Voluntary changes

FR-2.1 A voluntary change is one the passenger requests when their flight is
operating as scheduled. Change fees and any fare difference apply according to
the table in FR-1.

FR-2.2 BASIC_ECONOMY tickets cannot be changed voluntarily. The passenger must
buy a new ticket.

## FR-3 Involuntary changes

FR-3.1 An involuntary change is one caused by Meridian: a cancellation, a
significant schedule change, or a missed connection on a Meridian itinerary.

FR-3.2 **Fare rules do not apply to involuntary changes.** When Meridian
cancels a flight, the passenger is rebooked at no charge and no fare
difference is collected, regardless of fare class. This includes
BASIC_ECONOMY.

FR-3.3 A passenger who declines all offered alternatives after an involuntary
change is entitled to a refund of the unused portion, in the original form of
payment, regardless of whether the fare was sold as non-refundable. See
US-DOT-2 and EU261-4.
