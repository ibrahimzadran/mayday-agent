# EU261 Compensation

Summary of Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 as it applies to passengers. This is a
plain-language summary for agent use, not legal advice.

## EU261-1 When it applies

EU261-1.1 The regulation covers any flight **departing from an airport in the
EU**, on any airline, and any flight **arriving in the EU on an EU-based
airline**.

EU261-1.2 Flights departing the United Kingdom are covered by UK261 instead,
which is a separate regime with amounts in pounds. See UK261-1.

EU261-1.3 The passenger must have a confirmed reservation and have checked in
on time.

## EU261-2 Compensation amounts

EU261-2.1 For a cancellation, or an arrival delay of 3 hours or more:

| Flight distance | Compensation |
| --- | --- |
| 1,500 km or less | EUR 250 |
| Over 1,500 km within the EU | EUR 400 |
| Between 1,500 km and 3,500 km | EUR 400 |
| Over 3,500 km, not within the EU | EUR 600 |

EU261-2.2 Distance is measured from the first departure point to the final
destination, by great-circle distance.

## EU261-3 When compensation is reduced or not owed

EU261-3.1 Compensation is halved if the passenger is re-routed and arrives
within 2 hours (short haul), 3 hours (medium haul) or 4 hours (long haul) of
the original arrival time.

EU261-3.2 No compensation is owed if the passenger was told of the
cancellation **14 days or more** before departure.

EU261-3.3 No compensation is owed if the cancellation was caused by
extraordinary circumstances. See EXT-1.

## EU261-4 Refund or re-routing

EU261-4.1 Independently of compensation, the passenger may choose a full
refund within 7 days, or re-routing to their destination at the earliest
opportunity, or re-routing at a later date of their choosing.

## EU261-5 Right to care

EU261-5.1 Meals and refreshments, hotel accommodation where an overnight stay
is required, transport between airport and hotel, and two communications, are
owed **regardless of the cause**, including extraordinary circumstances.

## EU261-6 Compensation is separate from care

EU261-6.1 Vouchers, meals and hotels do not count against compensation.
Accepting them does not waive a claim.

EU261-6.2 An airline may not require a passenger to accept travel vouchers in
place of cash compensation.
