# Meal and Hotel Vouchers

## VOU-1 Meal vouchers

VOU-1.1 A meal voucher of USD 15 is issued to any passenger whose flight is
cancelled, or delayed 3 hours or more, and who is waiting at the airport.

VOU-1.2 Meal vouchers are issued for both controllable and uncontrollable
disruptions. Meridian does not means-test them and does not require the
passenger to ask.

VOU-1.3 One meal voucher per passenger per disruption. A passenger who asks
twice receives the same code again, not a second voucher.

## VOU-2 Who is eligible

VOU-2.1 Any fare class, including BASIC_ECONOMY. Vouchers are not a fare
benefit; they are disruption care.

VOU-2.2 Passengers who have already left the airport are not eligible.

## VOU-3 Hotel accommodation

VOU-3.1 A hotel voucher of USD 120 covers one room for one night, and is
issued when a disruption forces an overnight stay away from the passenger's
origin or home city.

VOU-3.2 For **controllable** disruptions Meridian pays for the room. For
**uncontrollable** disruptions Meridian offers a distressed-passenger rate but
the passenger pays.

VOU-3.3 Passengers stranded at their home city airport are not eligible for
hotel accommodation.

VOU-3.4 Ground transport to and from the hotel is included in both cases.

## VOU-4 What vouchers are not

VOU-4.1 A voucher is not compensation. Accepting a meal or hotel voucher does
not reduce or waive any statutory compensation owed under EU261, UK261, or any
other regulation. See EU261-6.
