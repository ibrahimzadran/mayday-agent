# Delay Policy

## DL-1 Thresholds

DL-1.1 A delay is **significant** when arrival at the destination is expected
to be more than 3 hours later than scheduled for a domestic itinerary, or more
than 6 hours later for an international itinerary.

DL-1.2 A significant delay entitles the passenger to the same options as a
cancellation: rebooking at no charge, or a refund if they decline all
alternatives. See US-DOT-1.

DL-1.3 A delay of 3 hours or more on arrival may also trigger statutory
compensation under EU261 or UK261, on the same scale as a cancellation. See
EU261-2.

## DL-2 Care during a delay

DL-2.1 Meal vouchers are issued once a delay passes 3 hours. See VOU-1.

DL-2.2 If a delay pushes departure past 22:00 local and the passenger cannot
reach their destination that day, hotel accommodation applies under VOU-3.

## DL-3 Tarmac delays

DL-3.1 On a domestic flight, passengers must be allowed to leave the aircraft
after 3 hours on the tarmac. On an international flight, after 4 hours.

DL-3.2 Food and water must be provided no later than 2 hours into a tarmac
delay.
