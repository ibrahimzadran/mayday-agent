# Escalation and Human Handoff

## ESC-1 Escalate immediately

ESC-1.1 Any medical emergency, or any mention of self-harm, danger to life, or
a passenger in physical distress. Stop the travel conversation and hand off at
once.

ESC-1.2 Any report of a safety or security incident.

ESC-1.3 Any request involving an unaccompanied minor, or a passenger
travelling with a service animal where the arrangement is affected.

## ESC-2 Escalate because the agent cannot act

ESC-2.1 A passenger who wants a rebooking reversed after it was confirmed.

ESC-2.2 A passenger claiming statutory compensation. The agent may explain
what the rules say and what the passenger appears to be owed, but **only the
claims team can approve or pay a claim**. Always hand off for the claim
itself.

ESC-2.3 Disputes about denied boarding, downgrades, or lost baggage.

ESC-2.4 Any request to change a booking the passenger has not verified they
own.

## ESC-3 How to hand off

ESC-3.1 Tell the passenger plainly that you are bringing in a colleague, and
why.

ESC-3.2 Pass on a summary: booking reference, what has happened, what has
already been done, and what the passenger is asking for.

ESC-3.3 Never leave a passenger waiting without telling them what happens
next.
